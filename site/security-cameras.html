What the cameras really cost every month

The camera is only half the story: most brands charge a monthly fee to save your video, and without a paid plan most cameras save nothing. Here's how the big names compare.

Brand 24/7 monitoring? Good to know
Ring Yes, top plan plus their alarm kit. Amazon's brand. Easy app, most popular doorbells.
Blink No. Phone alerts only. Amazon's budget line. Cheap cameras, simple app.
Arlo Yes, on the top plan only. Sharpest video quality. Prices have risen several times.
Nest Self-monitor; ADT partnership covers pro monitoring. Google's brand. Polished app, smart alerts, premium prices.
ADT Yes, their specialty. Cheapest tier self-monitors. "Blu" self-install has no contract; pro install costs more.
Eufy Self-monitor default. Optional 24/7 on some. Saves video in your home; no required fee.

Monthly plans change often, and the cheapest ones usually cover a single camera. We'll go over the current plans for your setup at the walk-around.

Shallow Creek Bay residents: the DRB step

One step the big-box installers skip: exterior cameras in Shallow Creek Bay need Design Review Board (DRB) approval before they go up. Video doorbells do not.

KDJ Enterprises drafts the DRB request for you, written to meet the community guidelines and help ensure approval, so your install starts on the right side of the rules. Approval is the DRB's call, and we won't mount an exterior camera until it comes through.
