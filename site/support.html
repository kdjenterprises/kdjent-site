What counts as support?

If it's covered by your plan and it's not working right, that's support. Slow computers, error messages, email problems, printer fights, "it worked yesterday," questions about something we set up for you. When in doubt, send it in. If it turns out to be outside your plan, we'll say so up front before any extra work or cost.
