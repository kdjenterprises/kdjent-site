Ladderly Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Ladderly is a word puzzle game made by KDJ Apps, a trade name of KDJ Enterprises LLC. This page explains what the app does with your information. The short version: it collects nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. Ladderly does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

What stays on your phone

Your puzzle progress, streaks, statistics, and settings are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Game Center

If you choose to sign in to Apple's Game Center, Ladderly posts your scores (rungs used on the daily ladder, your best streak, and your under-par solve count) to Game Center leaderboards under your Game Center nickname. Game Center is operated by Apple and is covered by Apple's privacy policy. You can turn this off in the app under Stats, Settings, Game Center, or by signing out of Game Center in your iPhone settings. We never see your Game Center account details.

Sharing results

The Share button creates a short text summary of a puzzle you solved and hands it to the iPhone share sheet. Where it goes from there is up to you.

Children

Ladderly is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
