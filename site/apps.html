Tallymile

Drive. It counts. Automatic mileage for taxes.
Coming soon to the App Store

Records every drive on its own, even with the app closed, and ends the trip when you park. Tap Business or Personal once, and future trips between the same two places sort themselves. At tax time it hands you monthly and yearly reports with the IRS deduction already figured.

How an app gets built

You don't need to know anything technical. You need to know what the app should do and who will use it. We handle the rest.

01

Scope and written quote

We talk through what the app needs to do, check the App Store for name and category conflicts, and send a written quote with what's included and what isn't. Nothing starts until you approve it.

02

A real build on your phone

Within the first week you install a working version through Apple's TestFlight and try it on your own phone. New builds land every few days, so you react to the real thing instead of mockups.

03

Real-world testing

Anything that uses location, the camera, notifications, or background work gets tested on physical phones, not just a simulator. For Tallymile that meant actual test drives.

04

Store listing, done for you

Screenshots, description, privacy page, support page, and Apple's review notes are all prepared and submitted. We handle Apple's questions during review, too.

Good to know before you ask

  • Apple's developer account (a yearly fee paid to Apple) is opened in your business's name, so you own the app, not us.
  • The quote lists what's included. Features added after approval are priced as a change order before we build them, never as a surprise on the invoice.
  • App Review takes time. Apple reviews every app before it goes live and often asks a question or two. Budget at least one round trip; we handle the back and forth.
  • iPhone first. Android is available on request and quoted separately.
