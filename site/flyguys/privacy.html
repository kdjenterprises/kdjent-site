Fly Guys Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Fly Guys is a fishing trip log made by KDJ Apps, a trade name of KDJ Enterprises LLC. This page explains what the app does with your information. The short version: there is no account, and everything you put in the app is kept on your phone. If you turn on sharing between phones, your group's trips, fish log, finished routes and star ratings are also kept in an online log that only your group's link opens. That is the one case where your information is stored anywhere but your phone.

What stays on your phone

Trips, photos you add, gear lists, practice lists, travel plans, reminders, your gear care list, the routes you record, your fish log and your settings are saved on your device. Unless you turn on sharing between phones (the next section), nothing is uploaded to KDJ Apps or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes what is on the phone. So does the Erase everything on this phone button on the More tab.

Sharing between phones (only if you turn it on)

Sharing is off until someone in your group starts a group on the More tab and sends the others a link, and each person taps Join. There is still no account, no email and no password. When it is on, this is what happens.

What goes into the shared log: the trips and what is written on them (the water, the town, the dates, who went, guests, the guide or fly shop, flies, best fish and notes), the flights and drives on each trip's travel board (who, airline, flight number, airports, dates and times), the gear and practice lists with who has ticked what, where the group stayed on trips that are already over, the fish log (who caught it, the kind, the length, the fly, a note, and where it was caught if it has a pin), and each finished day on the water (its route, distance, start and end time, and who recorded it). Star ratings go in without a name: the others see only the group's average and how many ratings it is made of. The name you picked under "Who's holding the phone" goes in so the group can see which phones have joined.

What never goes in: where the group will stay on a trip that has not happened yet, Google Photos album links, costs, your photos, reminders, your gear care list, your AI key and your other settings. Where your phone is right now is never shared: a day on the water goes in only after you tap Stop and save it, and fish marked during that day wait until then. A fish you log outside a recorded day goes in when you save it, with its pin if it has one.

Where it is kept and who can see it: the shared log is kept on Google's Firebase service (Cloud Firestore), in a project run by KDJ Enterprises LLC for this app. It is filed under a long random key that is part of your group's link. Anyone who has that link and the app can read and change your group's log, so keep the link to your group. Other groups cannot find or list yours. As the operator of the project, KDJ Enterprises is technically able to read what is stored in it. We do not look at it, sell it, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone, and we use it only to make sharing work.

The sign-in: to let your phone use the shared log, Firebase gives the app an anonymous sign-in: a random ID with no name, email, phone number or password. The app also gives your phone a random ID inside your group. Like any internet request, using the shared log shows your IP address to Google. Google's terms and privacy policy for Firebase apply to that service.

Stopping and deleting: Stop sharing on this phone (More tab, Sharing) stops your phone sending and receiving; what is on your phone stays, and what your phone already put in the shared log stays there for the rest of the group. Deleting a trip, a fish or a day while sharing removes it for the whole group. Make a new group link empties the old shared log and moves the group to a new one. Erase everything on this phone stops sharing first and does not touch the group's log. To have your group's shared log deleted entirely, email us from the Contact section below.

Your location

The app uses your location in two cases, and only when you start them yourself. When you tap Start on Day on the water, it records your route until you tap Stop, including while the screen is off, so it can draw the route and count the miles. When you mark a fish, it notes where the phone is so the fish gets a pin. The route and the pins are saved on your phone. They are not sent anywhere unless you have turned on sharing between phones, in which case a finished day and the fish log go into your group's shared log as described above. The app asks for location "While using the app" and never for "Always". A recording left running for 16 hours stops itself.

Photos and the camera

The app uses your photos or camera only when you tap Add photos or Camera. The photos you choose are kept inside the app on your phone. If a photo carries the place it was taken and your phone passes that along, the app keeps it on your phone so the photo can appear on that trip's map.

What leaves your phone, and when

  • App updates. When the app opens it asks Expo's update service whether a newer version is available and downloads it if so. That request carries a random ID for this copy of the app (not tied to you, your account or your device's hardware), the app's version details and, if the app failed to start, a short error note. Like any internet request it also shows your IP address to the service. Expo handles this on our behalf; see Expo's privacy policy. It is not used for advertising or tracking.
  • Maps. When you open a map in the app, your phone downloads the map pictures for that area from OpenStreetMap and the map's drawing code from the public code host unpkg.com. If a trip you typed in has a town but no place on the map yet, the town name (and nothing else) is sent to OpenStreetMap's search to find it. The Open in Maps button opens Apple Maps or Google Maps with the town name.
  • AI features, only if you set them up. "What fish is this?" and reading a flight confirmation need a key from your own Anthropic or OpenAI account, which you enter on the More tab. The key is kept in your phone's secure storage. When you tap the button, that photo or that confirmation text goes straight from your phone to the company whose key you entered, under your account with them. It does not pass through KDJ Apps. Without a key these features do nothing, and everything else works.
  • River and weather. When you open River and weather for a trip, the app sends that trip's map position (the town's spot on the map, not where you are) to three United States government services to get the numbers: the U.S. Geological Survey for river flow, the National Weather Service for the forecast, and NOAA for tides. Nothing else is sent. Sunrise and sunset are worked out on your phone.
  • Recap cards. A recap card is a picture the app makes on your phone. It goes wherever you send it from your phone's share sheet, and nowhere else.
  • Ask AI. The Ask AI buttons open the AI app or website you picked with a question the app wrote from your trip log. Nothing is sent until you tap the button.
  • Shared albums and links. Album buttons and web links open in Google Photos or your browser.

What we collect

KDJ Apps has no accounts, no advertising and no tracking. If you never turn on sharing between phones, the only information collected through the app is what the update service described above receives. If you do turn it on, your group's shared log is stored as described in the sharing section above, and nothing else is collected.

Reminders

Trip and gear care reminders are local notifications scheduled by the app on your phone. No outside notification service is involved.

Children

Fly Guys is not directed at children and has no accounts or profiles.
