Fly Guys Support

A fishing trip log, by KDJ Apps

Need help? Email Support@kdjent.com and we will get back to you.

Getting started

Open Fly Guys and tap your name under Who's holding the phone? The Trips tab shows the countdown to the next trip and one card per year. Tap a card to open that trip.

Adding or fixing a trip

Tap the red + button on the Trips tab to add a trip, or the dashed Add the trip slot on an empty year. To change a trip, open it and tap Edit at the top right. Add the First day and Last day to a planned trip and the countdown starts.

Photos

Open a trip and tap Add photos or Camera. The Open the shared album button opens that year's shared album. To set or change an album link, go to the Photos tab and tap Link album or the pencil next to the year.

Getting ready for a trip

Open the next trip. Travel board holds everyone's flights and drives. Gear to pack is the packing list. Practice is what to work on before you go. Gear care is what to look after before and after the trip. Reminders turns on reminders for this phone.

Reminders are not showing up

  • Open the next trip, tap Reminders, and check the switch at the top is on.
  • If the app says notifications are turned off, tap Open phone settings and allow notifications for Fly Guys.
  • Reminders need dates. Add the trip's first day, or your flight on the Travel board.

"What fish is this?" and reading a flight confirmation

These two features are optional and need a key from your own Anthropic or OpenAI account, entered on the More tab under AI helper. Without a key you can still name a fish by hand and type a flight in.

Your data

Everything stays on your phone. There is no account. See the privacy policy.
