Getting started

Open Fly Guys and tap your name under Who's holding the phone? The Trips tab shows the countdown to the next trip and one card per year. Tap a card to open that trip.

Adding or fixing a trip

Tap the red + button on the Trips tab to add a trip, or the dashed Add the trip slot on an empty year. To change a trip, open it and tap Edit at the top right. Add the First day and Last day to a planned trip and the countdown starts.

A day on the water

On the Trips tab, tap Day on the water, then Start the day. When the phone asks about location, choose While using the app. The app keeps drawing your route with the screen off. Tap Mark a fish each time someone lands one, then Save this fish. Tap Stop at the end of the day to save it. From the saved day you can open the Recap card and send it to the group.

  • If the route has gaps, some phones stop apps in the background to save battery. Allow Fly Guys to run in the background in your phone's battery settings.
  • A day left running for 16 hours stops itself.

Fish log and fly box

The Fish log on the Trips tab keeps score: who caught the most, the biggest fish and the most kinds, per trip or all time. The Fly box shows which flies worked on each water, from the fish you log.

Photos and album links

Open a trip and tap Add photos. Photos you add stay on your phone. Each year's shared Google Photos album opens with Open the shared album on the trip, or from the Photos tab.

To get the album links on a new phone: someone who already has them goes to the Photos tab, taps Album links, then Send as a message. On the new phone, tap the link in that message, then tap the Add button. If tapping the link does nothing, copy the whole message, open Album links on the new phone and tap Paste.

Sharing one log between phones

Sharing is off until your group turns it on, and it needs no account or password. One person goes to the More tab, opens Sharing between phones, taps Start the group, then Send the group link. Everyone else taps that link on the phone that has Fly Guys and taps Join.

  • Keep the group link to your group. Anyone with the link and the app can see and change your group's log.
  • To leave, tap Stop sharing on this phone on the same screen. What is on your phone stays.
  • If the link got out, tap Make a new group link and send the new one to the group.

Getting ready for a trip

Open the next trip. Travel board holds everyone's flights and drives. Gear to pack is the packing list. Practice is what to work on before you go. Gear care is what to look after before and after the trip. River and weather shows river flow, the forecast and tides for the trip. Reminders turns on reminders for this phone.

Reminders are not showing up

  • Open the next trip, tap Reminders, and check the switch at the top is on.
  • If the app says notifications are turned off, tap Open phone settings and allow notifications for Fly Guys.
  • Reminders need dates. Add the trip's first day, or your flight on the Travel board.

"What fish is this?" and reading a flight confirmation

These two features are optional and need a key from your own Anthropic or OpenAI account, entered on the More tab under AI helper. Without a key you can still name a fish by hand and type a flight in.

Merch

The Merch tab shows the group's store. Orders, shipping and returns are handled by the store itself.

Your data

There is no account. Everything you add is kept on your phone. If your group turns on sharing between phones, your group's trips, fish log and finished days are also kept in a shared log that only your group's link opens. The privacy policy has the details.
