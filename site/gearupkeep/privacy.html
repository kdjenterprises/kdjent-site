Gear Upkeep Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026. Gear Upkeep is made by KDJ Enterprises LLC ("KDJ Apps").

Gear Upkeep is a maintenance tracker for the equipment around your home. The short version: your data stays on your phone, we have no servers, no accounts, and no analytics, and we never see anything you put in the app.

What the app stores

Equipment details (names, brands, model and serial numbers, hour meter readings, notes), photos you attach, maintenance tasks, the service log, your shopping stores, reminder settings, and seasonal checklist progress. All of it is saved on your device only. It is not sent to KDJ Apps or to anyone else by the app. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

Optional AI features and your own API key

Gear Upkeep can use an artificial intelligence service to read equipment tags and find parts prices. This is optional and off until you set it up. There are two ways to use it:

  • Ask AI. The app opens ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity or Google in your browser or their app with a question already written. What happens there is governed by that service's own privacy policy. Nothing is sent by Gear Upkeep itself.
  • Your own API key. If you choose to paste an API key from your own Anthropic or OpenAI account into Settings, the app sends your requests directly from your phone to that provider: the photo of an equipment tag when you use Scan the model tag, and the part name, part number, equipment brand and model, and your chosen store names when you use Search with my key. The provider processes that data under its own terms and privacy policy, and you pay the provider directly. KDJ Apps is not involved in that connection and cannot see it.

Your API key is stored in the iPhone's secure keychain on your device. It is never sent anywhere except to the provider you chose. You can remove it at any time in Settings.

Camera and photos

The app asks for camera or photo library access only when you choose to attach a photo or scan a tag. Photos you attach are copied into the app's private storage on your device. They leave your device only if you use Scan the model tag with your own API key, as described above.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules notifications on your device for upcoming maintenance. These are local notifications; no notification server is involved.

Links to stores

"Find it" opens a store's website (such as Amazon, Walmart, Lowe's or Home Depot) in your browser with a search for the part. Those sites have their own privacy policies. Gear Upkeep does not include affiliate links or tracking.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising.
  • No data sent to KDJ Apps.
  • No selling or sharing of your information.

Children

Gear Upkeep is a general-audience utility and does not knowingly collect any information from children.
