ABOUT KDJ

Twenty-five years of enterprise IT, pointed at small business.

KDJ Enterprises exists because the discipline that keeps global manufacturers running, monitored systems, tested backups, real security, is rarely offered to small businesses at a price that makes sense. We close that gap.

The guy behind it

KDJ is run by me, Kory Johnson, from right here in Murrells Inlet, South Carolina. I have spent my entire career in enterprise technology, and I started the same way I work today: hands on. Early on I personally deployed more than 60 ERP systems for companies around the world, managing the servers, the databases, and everything in between.

From there I was recruited to build a cloud software offering from the ground up. I ran the operations behind its first 10,000 users. Later, as a regional CTO for a national managed IT services provider, I advised companies of every size on hardening their IT operations. Today I lead managed services organizations supporting some of the largest manufacturers in North and Latin America.

Big companies pay a lot of money for that experience. Your business gets it by sending one email.

Why the red and gold

Before the tech career, I served in the North Dakota Army National Guard with the 188th Air Defense Artillery. The colors in the KDJ logo are a nod to the ADA branch colors, scarlet and gold.

Air Defense has one job: watch the sky and protect everything behind you. That is exactly how we approach IT. Watch the threats, guard the systems, and make sure the people counting on you never have to think about it.
