Getting started

Open Tallymile and tap Turn on automatic tracking. iPhone asks for location twice: choose Allow While Using App, then Change to Always Allow. Keep Precise on. That is all. Drive, and trips record themselves.

A trip did not record

  • Open the app and check the card at the top of Trips. It should say Tracking is on.
  • Go to Settings in the app. Location permission should say Always. If it says While Using, tap Open Settings, tap Location, and choose Always.
  • Trips shorter than a quarter mile, or that never reach 10 mph, are skipped on purpose.
  • The trip ends about four minutes after you park, so give it a few minutes before looking.
  • You can always add a missed trip by hand with the + button on the Trips tab.

Sorting trips

New trips land under Needs sorting. Tap Business or Personal. Name the places you drive between (open a trip and tap Name it), and once you sort a trip between two named places, future trips between them sort themselves.

Reports

The Reports tab shows each month and the full year. PDF report makes a document for your tax preparer; Spreadsheet makes a CSV you can open in Excel or Numbers. Both go out through the iPhone share sheet.

IRS mileage rate

Deductions use the IRS standard mileage rate in force on each trip's date. The rate table is in Settings; when the IRS announces a new year's rate, add it there. Tallymile is a record keeper, not tax advice.

Your data

Everything stays on your phone. There is no account and nothing is uploaded. Export a yearly spreadsheet now and then as a backup. See the privacy policy.
