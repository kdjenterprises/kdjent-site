Tallymile Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Tallymile is a mileage tracker made by KDJ Apps, a trade name of KDJ Enterprises LLC. This page explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your location and your trips stay on your phone, and we never see them.

What we collect

Nothing. Tallymile has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. The app never sends your location, your trips, or anything else to KDJ Apps. The only times anything leaves your phone are the two Apple and Google map features described below.

Location

Tallymile asks for "Always" location permission so it can notice when you start driving and record the trip while the app is closed. Location is processed entirely on your phone: the app turns GPS readings into a trip with a distance, a start point and an end point, and saves that trip on the device. Nothing is uploaded. You can turn tracking off in the app at any time, or change the permission in iPhone Settings, and the app keeps working for trips you add by hand.

Addresses

To show a street address instead of map coordinates for where a trip started and ended, the app uses the address lookup built into iOS. That request is handled by Apple and covered by Apple's privacy policy. You can replace addresses with your own place names (such as "Home" or "Office") inside the app.

Maps

A recorded trip can show its route on a map drawn by Apple Maps. To draw the map, your phone asks Apple for the map of that area. That request is handled by Apple and covered by Apple's privacy policy. The trip screen also has Apple Maps and Google Maps buttons. They do nothing until you tap one; tapping opens that map app with the trip's start and end points so it can show directions. If you choose Google Maps, those two points are sent to Google and covered by Google's privacy policy.

What stays on your phone

Your trips, places, sorting rules, vehicle details, odometer readings and settings are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. The Erase all data button in Settings does the same.

Reports you export

When you export a PDF or spreadsheet, the file is created on your phone and handed to the iPhone share sheet. Where it goes from there (email, Files, AirDrop) is up to you.

Notifications

"Trip recorded" alerts are local notifications scheduled by the app on your phone. No notification service outside your device is involved.

Children

Tallymile is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.
